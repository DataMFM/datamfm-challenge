Dev Phase

The development phase allows participants to evaluate their models on the dev split of the OmniDocBench dataset.

Details

  • Dataset: Dev split (80% of OmniDocBench pages)
  • Submissions: 10 per day, 100 per month
  • Leaderboard: Public — all scores are visible
  • Format: .zip containing .md files (one per page)

Use this phase to develop, debug, and iterate on your model. Scores are computed on the dev split and shown on the public leaderboard.

Terms and Conditions

  1. Eligibility: The challenge is open to all researchers worldwide. There is no team size limit. Individual participants are welcome.
  2. Submissions: Participants must submit predictions in the specified format (.zip containing .md files). Submissions that do not follow the format will receive a score of 0.
  3. Fair Use: Participants must not use the ground truth test annotations in any form during model development or inference.
  4. External Data: Use of external training data is allowed but must be disclosed in the submission metadata and technical report.
  5. Technical Report: Top-ranked teams (as determined by the organizers) must submit a technical report describing their method to be eligible for awards.
  6. Multiple Accounts: Each team should use a single account. Using multiple accounts to circumvent submission limits will result in disqualification.
  7. Results: By participating, you agree that your team name and scores may be published in workshop proceedings and on the challenge website.
  8. Organizer Rights: The organizers reserve the right to disqualify any submission that violates these terms or the spirit of fair competition.

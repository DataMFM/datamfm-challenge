Terms and Conditions

  1. Eligibility: The challenge is open to all researchers worldwide. There is no team size limit. Individual participants are welcome.
  2. Task selection: Participants must select the correct Task metadata value for each submission. Dev Phase and Test Phase select the evaluation split; Task selects the evaluator.
  3. Submission format: Participants must submit predictions as a .zip archive in the format required by the selected Task. Submissions that do not follow the selected task format may fail evaluation.
  4. Fair use: Participants must not use hidden ground truth annotations in any form during model development or inference.
  5. External data: Use of external training data is allowed but must be disclosed in the submission metadata and technical report.
  6. Technical report: Top-ranked teams, as determined by the organizers, must submit a technical report describing their method to be eligible for awards.
  7. Multiple accounts: Each team should use a single account. Using multiple accounts to circumvent submission limits may result in disqualification.
  8. Results: By participating, you agree that your team name, selected task, and scores may be published in workshop proceedings and on the challenge website.
  9. Organizer rights: The organizers reserve the right to disqualify any submission that violates these terms or the spirit of fair competition.
